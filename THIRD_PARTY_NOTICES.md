# Third-party data notices

## Openjam vocabulary database

The English headwords, Persian translations, topic categories, and related lexical metadata used by the LoghatBaz 6,000-word curriculum are retrieved from Openjam:

- Project: https://github.com/amirj4m/openjam
- API: https://openjam.amirj4m.com
- License: MIT
- Dataset described by the upstream integration guide: 19,282 English lemmas, 35 topical categories, and 32,600 Persian translations (dataset v0.7.0).

The upstream project notes that translations are AI-generated and may contain errors; they have not all been human-reviewed. LoghatBaz validates the curriculum structure and removes duplicate English headwords, but this does not replace linguistic review of every translation. Corrections should be reviewed before a commercial public release.
